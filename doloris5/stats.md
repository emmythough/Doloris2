This section, labeled Example 6.6: Population Variance Example, directly applies the definitional formula for population variance to the fair die example (Example 6.4, where the mean $\mu=3.5$).The objective is to compute the variance $\sigma^2 = E[(X-\mu)^2] = \sum_{i=1}^{N} p_{i}(x_{i}-\mu)^{2}$.Example 6.6: Population Variance Calculation (Fair Die)The calculation is shown in a tabular format, which is often useful for tracking intermediate steps.Step 1: Set up the Table and Calculate Squared Deviations $(x-\mu)^2$The calculation begins by noting that the population mean is $\mu = 3.5$.
